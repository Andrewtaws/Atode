Welcome to Atode

Worrying all day keeps your brain on high alert. Giving worries a set time teaches your brain they can wait.

Hand one over, and collect it later, like a coat in a cloakroom.

A technique from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT).

How it works

  1. 1

    Notice it

    A worry pops up while you're busy.

  2. 2

    Park it

    One tap. You get a ticket, and go back to what you were doing.

  3. 3

    Collect it at your time

    Look at each one for a moment. Many will have faded by then, and that's the point.
