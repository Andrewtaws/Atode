Motion

Soft and small. When the phone is set to reduce motion, movement switches off and screens only fade.

  • Loading screen: logo rises in, yellow bar fills, then it fades away (about 1.3s, every time the app opens).
  • Screen changes: the old screen fades out (--motion-leave, 180ms), then the new one fades and slides in 12px, from the right going forward and from the left going back (--motion-page, 480ms). With reduce motion it only fades.
  • Ticket: lands with a slight spring just after its screen (--motion-slow, 420ms).
  • Presses and choices: buttons and tiles shrink to 98% when pressed, the selected tick pops in, and colours ease (--motion-fast, 150ms).
  • Errors: a short side-to-side nudge when a message appears.

Changes from Figma

Made so every screen meets the project rules: text 14px or larger, 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for controls.

  • Text at 12px and 13px in Figma (topic chip, card labels, "Reminder on", note dates) → 14px.
  • Text box border #d0d0d0 (1.5:1) → #767676 (4.5:1), so the box is visible.
  • Choice pills 46px → 48px tall, and Back and "Review notes early" get a 44px tap area without moving.
  • Selected choices, tiles and pills get a tick as well as the black fill.
  • "This can't wait" stays top right on every screen (not in the new Figma frames).
  • Review Notes keeps Back, the "Reviewed notes" tab and "I’ll look at these notes tomorrow", so there's always a way out.
  • "Whats" and "Its" → "What’s" and "It’s".
